Market awareness

Know when a record changed without turning the app into a shop.

Wantlist and value changes are treated as collection context. Groovie can surface price movement, available copies and missing releases without burying the user in marketplace noise.

Data model

Discogs-powered, collector-owned.

Discogs metadata. Groovie is built around the release information collectors already maintain in Discogs.

Personal layer. Notes, folders, condition and listening intent are treated as the user’s collection layer.

Native workflow. The interface is designed for fast checks in record stores and slower collection review at home.
